BIO

TACGRU Tactical Combat Application Group is an Indonesian‑based Arma Reforger milsim community inspired by the structure, doctrine, and operational mindset of U.S. Special Operations Forces. Built around Tier 1 and Tier 2 concepts, TACGRU brings together specialized ground elements, aviation, reconnaissance, and joint capabilities to create immersive and challenging PvE operations. Our focus is simple: realistic teamwork, disciplined execution, and an experience that goes beyond the ordinary battlefield.

OUR APPROACH

Every operation begins with a plan, but success depends on the team behind it. TACGRU emphasizes communication, preparation, coordination, and adaptability. From Ranger‑style direct action to special mission operations and aviation support, every element operates as part of a larger task force.

SERE QUALIFICATION

Earn the right to operate. SERE Qualification is a specialized training and assessment program designed for operators working into the JSOC element. Candidates are challenged through simulated SERE curriculum focused on survival, evasion, resistance, and escape — conducted under adverse conditions.

The program is developed and delivered under the guidance of an experienced real‑world SERE instructor, ensuring realistic knowledge and training methodology within the TACGRU roleplay environment.

FROM CANDIDATE TO OPERATOR

SERE Qualification is a hallmark achievement toward the TACGRU/JSOC element, and demonstrates the discipline, adaptability, and resilience expected from a special operator.

QUALIFICATION INCLUDES

  • Survival & Fieldcraft
  • Evasion Techniques
  • Resistance Principles
  • Land Navigation
  • Environmental Awareness
  • Stress & Decision Making
  • Communications & Reporting
  • Team‑Based SERE Scenarios
  • Final Field Evaluation
